### Cambodia Jobs

#### Job Portals

- [BongThom](https://www.bongthom.com) - Leading job portal in Cambodia specializing in corporate, NGO, and technical vacancies. *(Jul 2026)*
- [CamHR](https://www.camhr.com) - Popular job search engine and employment platform for commercial and corporate positions. *(Jul 2026)*
- [Cam-Jobs](https://www.cam-jobs.com) - Khmer language job portal and CV submission database for local employment. *(Jul 2026)*
- [JobNet Cambodia](https://www.jobnet.com.kh) - Job vacancy marketplace covering various professional industries across Cambodia. *(Jul 2026)*

#### Telegram Channels and Groups

- [IT Jobs Cambodia (Telegram)](https://t.me/itjobscambodia) - Largest Telegram community for software engineering, IT support, and tech vacancies in Cambodia. *(Jul 2026)*
- [Cambodia HR & Jobs (Telegram)](https://t.me/cambodiajobss) - Active daily job channel broadcasting corporate, sales, and administrative vacancies. *(Jul 2026)*
- [DevCambodia Community (Telegram)](https://t.me/devcambodia) - Developer chat group sharing technical job openings and peer code reviews. *(Jul 2026)*
- [Job Search Cambodia (Telegram)](https://t.me/jobsearchcambodia) - Daily job broadcast channel covering Phnom Penh and provincial vacancies. *(Jul 2026)*

#### Facebook Groups and Communities

- [Job Announcement in Cambodia](https://www.facebook.com/groups/jobincambodia) - Major Facebook group featuring hundreds of daily employer hiring posts across Cambodia. *(Jul 2026)*
- [IT & Tech Jobs Phnom Penh](https://www.facebook.com/groups/itjobscambodia) - Specialized Facebook community for technology, UI/UX design, and digital marketing positions. *(Jul 2026)*
- [NGO Jobs Cambodia Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/ngojobscambodia) - Community group dedicated to non-profit, development, and community project hiring. *(Jul 2026)*

#### Government and Civil Service

- [Ministry of Civil Service](https://mcs.gov.kh) - Official state ministry managing public sector appointments and civil service examinations. *(Jul 2026)*
- [Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training](https://www.mlvt.gov.kh) - Government ministry regulating employment contracts, labor rights, and workplace safety standards. *(Mar 2025)*
- [National Employment Agency (NEA)](https://www.nea.gov.kh) - Official Cambodian government portal providing public placement services and workforce guidance. *(Nov 2022)*

- [ReliefWeb Cambodia](https://reliefweb.int/country/khm) - Specialized humanitarian and development sector job listings for international aid agencies. *(Jul 2026)*
- [UN Jobs Cambodia](https://cambodia.un.org) - Official United Nations portal listing agency vacancies across Cambodia. *(Jun 2026)*
